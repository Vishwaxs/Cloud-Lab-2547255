========================================================================
SELF-LEARNING CONCEPT 1: class-imbalance handling + threshold tuning
========================================================================
RandomForest + SMOTE         ROC-AUC=0.728  PR-AUC=0.195  F1=0.183  P=0.191  R=0.175
SMOTE sits INSIDE the pipeline, so it is re-fitted per CV fold and never
synthesises neighbours of validation rows. Applying it before the split is
the standard leakage mistake.

Every model above is reported at its OWN threshold, tuned on the training
F1 curve only. Without this, well-ranked models that emit low absolute
probabilities look broken at the arbitrary 0.5 cut-off (an unregularised
RandomForest scored F1=0.03 that way while still ranking cases sensibly).
